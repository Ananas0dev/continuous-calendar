Those services and libraries retain their own terms and licenses; their remote responses are not bundled here. Review them before replacing external URLs or distributing additional assets.

Complete edit history was unavailable, so the precise reused/rewritten boundary cannot be established. No blanket ownership claim is made over third-party code. A license for new project-specific additions has not been selected; the npm package is marked UNLICENSED rather than silently extending an upstream license to unrelated material.
